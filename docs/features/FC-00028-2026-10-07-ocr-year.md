# FC-00028 — Remove the hardcoded year from OCR

**Date:** 2026-10-07 · **Slice:** S · **Risk:** Medium · **Status:** done · **Affected entities:** All

## What
Timecard and EasyClocking OCR no longer assume 2026. The prompts carry the pay week's own dates (or today's date and the New Year rule when no schedule is loaded), and every OCR'd date that is clearly in the wrong year is moved to the nearest plausible year and flagged for review.

## Why
Both prompts said the year is 2026. Starting with the week of Dec 27 2026 – Jan 2 2027, every January punch would have been dated a year early, landed outside the week, and been silently dropped from payroll by the week filter.

## Fix
- `_ocrYearGuidance(scheduleWeek)` — prompt text built from the schedule's ISO dates (both years named when the week crosses New Year), or from today's date plus "a December date read in January belongs to the previous year".
- `tcPrompt`: stamp prefix explained generically ("26" = 2026, "27" = 2027; read it, never assume).
- `ecPrompt`: date format is `YYYY-MM-DD` with the year from the guidance.
- `_correctOcrYear(iso, refIso)` — if a date is more than 120 days from the reference (mid-week date, else today), move it to the closest of reference-year −1 / 0 / +1. Applied in `processReviewRow` to TC/EC rows only; manual and imported rows are never touched.
- Corrected rows carry the flag `Year corrected (OCR read YYYY) — verify date`, so they need review and the correction is visible.
- `_setOcrTodayForTests` exists so tests can pin "today".

## Where in UI
Actuals Intake review table (new flag text only).

## Touches
ocr

## Risk
Medium — touches every OCR'd date. Dates within 120 days of the reference are left exactly as read.

## Reversibility
Fully reversible via one revert commit.

## Definition of Done
1. No literal year in either OCR prompt.
2. A week spanning New Year produces correctly dated rows on both sides of Jan 1.
3. A clearly wrong year is corrected and flagged; correct dates are untouched.
4. Manual (MN) and imported (XL / re-import) rows are never year-corrected.
5. Existing tests pass; new tests pass.

## Out of scope
Schedule-screenshot OCR (its prompt never had a year). Multi-week images.

## Assumptions
1. 120 days is far enough from any real pay-week date to only catch year misreads.
